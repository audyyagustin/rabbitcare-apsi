# RabbitCare – Rabbit Clinic & Care Management System

A business and system analysis project for RabbitCare, a rabbit clinic and care management prototype. This project focuses on analyzing business processes, defining system requirements, and modeling workflows to support rabbit clinic operations and care management.

## Project Overview

RabbitCare was developed as an academic group project for the Information Systems Analysis and Design (APSI) course.

The project aims to identify business needs, analyze operational workflows, and translate business requirements into structured system models and documentation.

## Project Scope

The project covers the analysis and modeling of several business processes, including:

- Pet registration and profile management.
- Appointment booking and payment processes.
- Administrative service workflows.
- Doctor examination processes.
- Grooming and referral processes.
- Payment validation.
- Calendar, history, and reminder management.
- Manager reporting.
